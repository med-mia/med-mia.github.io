What is diabetes?

Diabetes means there is too much sugar in your blood.

You can feel well and still have high sugar, so keep taking your medicine. High sugar over many years can damage your eyes, kidneys, feet and heart.

✓ You can protect yourself.

Your care matters

Treatment

  • Take your medicine every day, even when you feel well.
  • Do not stop or change your tablets or insulin without speaking to the clinic.
  • Come for your check-ups and bring your medicine.
  • Tell us if something worries you, or if you cannot afford to come.

Know the signs

When to get help

Low sugar

Shaky, sweaty, very hungry or confused

Take sugar in water now, then eat.

High sugar

Very thirsty, passing urine a lot, tired or blurred vision

Come to the clinic.

Go to the clinic or hospital today

Get urgent help if you are very drowsy or confused, cannot swallow, are vomiting and cannot keep fluids down, have fast breathing, or have a foot sore, blister or wound that is not healing.
